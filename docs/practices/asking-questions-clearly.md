!!! QUOTE
    Its only a silly question if its not asked

!!! QUOTE
    An example would be good right now - when the challenge or issue is not clear or hard to describe


Asking clear questions allows people to provide relevant opinions and answers quickly

Virtuous Cycle: Practice asking clear questions grows confidence and speed in asking clear questions


## The Question cycle

**Write** > **Read** > **Refactor**

Write
- Describing the question well often leads to solving the challenge yourself

Read:
- Do you understand the question when you read it back to yourself


Refactor:
- Can the question be made even clearer


## Context
- include background, e.g. what is the situation driving the need for the question
- a clear example can be worth many additional words


## Challenges:

I don't have time, I just need a quick answer

- are you moving too fast?
- for successive occurrences, are you masking a bigger challenge or gap in knowledge
- effective engineering is a marathon (or a hike) rather than a sprint

I should know the answer to x:

- why should you know? Are you sure other people do not have this or similar gaps in their knowledge
- knowledge is leared and retained over time and also fades over time when not used
- Griffin has many unique concepts and designs, with a significantly large and evolving code base
