# Establish A Common Language

Even when people use the same natural language (E.g. English, Spanish) to communicate, there is no guarantee that thee is a shared understanding.


## Answer With Repetition

When agreeing on a course of action, each person should restated the action as they understand it, using words and terms they are highly confident of their meaning.


## Data Dictionary

A classic approach to capturing and sharing concepts in a form that is highly discoverable and simple to consume.

A Wiki or similar knowledge base tool (Static Site Generator, Notion, Atlassian Confluence, etc) are excellent starting points for a data dictionary.

Provide contribution guidelines and carry out submission reviews to maintain the integrity and value of your data dictionary.

??? TIP "Instant Messaging tools are rarely a structured knowledge base"
    The flexible and convenient approach to sharing in tools such as Slack/Discord rarely encourage a strong sense of long term discoverability.

    The information shared in these tools can be scraped for valuable information and added to a structured approach to knowledge.


## Anacronyms

Used to save time in verbal and written communication.

Only affective if there meaning is well defined and used within a specific context.

Every Anacronym should be included in a [data dictionary](#data-dictionary), covering the use across the whole organisation.
