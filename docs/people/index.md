## Notable People

People who have made a significant impact in the world of computer science and software engineering (non-exhaustive list).

- Ada Lovelace - inventor of computer programming
- [Charles Babbage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Babbage){target=_blank} - inventor of the digital programmable computer
- [Grace Hopper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grace_Hopper){target=_blank} - devise the theory of "machine-independent programming languages", influencing the creation of COBOL which accelerated the adoption of computing for business systems
- [Doug Englebart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Engelbart){target=_blank} - creator of [The Mother of all Demos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mother_of_All_Demos){target=_blank}
- [Ted Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Nelson){target=_blank} - created Project Xanadu (the Internet before the World Wide Web), coining the terms "Hypertext" and "Hypermedia"
- [Margaret Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Hamilton_(software_engineer)){target=_blank} - led the Apollo flight software team and coined the term "Software Engineering", making the world view software as a serious engineering discipline.
- [John McCarty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McCarthy_(computer_scientist)){target=_blank} invented Lisp programming language and co-founded the field of Artificial Intelligence (1956)
- [Linus Torvald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linus_Torvalds){target=_blank} - boosting the adoption of open source software via Linux
- [Bret Victor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bret_Victor){target=_blank} - driving creativity and the value of fast feedback with inventing on principle
- [Rich Hickey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rich_Hickey){target=_blank} - advocating design simplicity and programming fun through the Clojure programming language, a lisp for the 21st century


## Notable organisations

- Debian Linux - a long standing organisation championing Free Open Source Software and stewarding the longest running and most simple to use Linux Operating System distribution available today.
- [FreeDesktop.org (XDG)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedesktop.org){target=_blank} - supporting growth and reach of Open Source Software by maintaining standards and stewardship of projects including X11 / Wayland and tools for Unix-like operating systems
